So `<div class="callout">This has <b>bold</b>, a <a>link</a>, and <code>code</code>.</div>` now yields `> This has **bold**, a [link](url), and \`code\`.` — one line, markup intact. Multi-paragraph callouts still quote correctly. (This also improved real `<blockquote>` handling the same way.)
